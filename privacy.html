Privacy Policy

This page is a simple gateway: its only job is to take you to our Telegram channel. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum.

What we collect

No registration is required, and we never ask for personal data (name, email, phone number). Some technical details — browser type, device model, IP address — may be recorded automatically by our hosting provider and traffic-statistics services. This exists purely to keep the page running.

Cookies

The site uses only cookies it needs to function, plus anonymous analytics. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Third-party services

Clicking the button takes you to Telegram, where your data is handled under Telegram's own Privacy Policy. We do not control third-party platforms and are not responsible for how they process data.

Facebook Pixel (Meta)

To measure how well our ads perform, this site uses the Facebook Pixel (Meta Pixel) — an analytics tool provided by Meta. It may record technical details of your visit: pages viewed, time spent on the site, button clicks, and your device and browser type.

This data is passed to Meta and may be used for ad analytics and remarketing in line with Meta's Privacy Policy. You can opt out through your browser settings or your Facebook account.

Your rights

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. If you have questions about how your data is handled, contact us on Telegram.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
