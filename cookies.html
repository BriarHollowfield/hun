Cookie Policy

Cookies are tiny files a website stores in your browser. We keep them to a minimum: only what the page needs to work, plus anonymous statistics.

Which cookies we use

TypePurpose
Strictly necessaryRequired for the basic operation of the page. The site cannot function correctly without them, so they cannot be disabled.
AnalyticsAnonymised visit statistics: page views, device types. They help us understand how well the page performs.
Facebook Pixel (Meta)Ad & analytics cookies by Meta: measuring ad campaign performance, anonymised on-site actions, remarketing.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings (the "Privacy" section). The main function of this page — the Telegram button — works fine without them.

Facebook Pixel (Meta)

To measure ad performance, the page loads Meta's pixel code. It sets ad and analytics cookies and records anonymised actions: page views, button clicks, device type.

This data is handled by Meta under its Privacy Policy. You can opt out in your browser settings or your Facebook account.

Third-party cookies

Third-party services — including the Meta pixel and Telegram — may set their own cookies under their own policies. We have no access to those files and do not control them.
